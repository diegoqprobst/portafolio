# Design System: Umbrella — diegoquinde.com root

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Two-Ink Exhibit Booklet"**

This system is a printed object rendered in a browser: the mid-century technical
exhibit booklet (Union Carbide, *Giants of the New Age*, 1933) that the user
pinned with reference scans. A near-white ivory litho stock carries the page —
the booklet's own interior stock, where the paper stays light and the ink does
all the colour work — and it prints in **two inks**: a dusty periwinkle that
owns whole full-bleed fields, and a soft brick second ink that marks the
document — register rules, section numerals, bullet dashes, state squares, link underlines,
plate tabs, the mis-struck wordmark, and both calls to action. The first ink is a
value the user handed over by hex, and it is deliberately **light enough to read
as a printed colour rather than as a near-black**; it sits close to the ink on
the 1933 cover itself. A **white coated sheet** is bound inside the ivory stock:
the specification band and the moving-plate section sit on pure white, and white
is the knockout on every orange field. Type is a two-voice pairing: Montserrat
Black in tight all-caps for every display line, Helvetica Neue for every word of
body, caption, table cell and control. Structure is a ruled catalog grid: a 3px
orange register rule at each section break, hairline-ruled spec tables, figure
captions set under titles, a Contents index with dot leaders that earns the
01–07 section numbering. There are no cards, no radii, no shadows, no gradients,
no glow and no monospace anywhere in the shipped stylesheet.

**Scope — read this before changing anything.** This world governs **only the
umbrella root `/`**, and only inside the `.umbra` subtree (`app/umbrella.css`,
`app/UmbrellaClient.tsx`, `app/PlatePlayer.tsx`). Every other route — `/lumen`,
`/saratoga`, `/luxarmonie`, `/tools/lumen-calculator`, `/privacidad` and the
private `/admin` — still runs the previous system: `globals.css` + `lumen.css`,
Nunito / Fraunces / Hanken Grotesk / JetBrains Mono, dark indigo ground, amber
accent. That split is **deliberate and in-scope-by-exclusion**: the user left
those routes on the old system on purpose. Do not "unify" them, do not port
these tokens into `globals.css`, and do not treat the old system as drift to be
repaired. Three isolation rules exist for the same reason and must survive:
`.umbra :where(a)` keeps the link reset at specificity 0 so components set their
own colour, `.umbra #contact.u-close` exists because `globals.css` paints
`#contact` by id for the Lumen home and an id outranks any class, and
`html:has(.umbra)` kills the Lumen scroll-snap and repaints the document
background to the ink.

Depth comes from ink coverage and mis-registration, never from elevation. The
world's own device for "something moved" is a plate struck slightly off: an
orange plate 3px behind the wordmark, a 6px orange run slipped −3px under the
masthead, a black-halftone plate that slides 5px onto its ink mount and goes
full colour on hover.
Motion is one grammar, press-derived: the register rule sweeps left to right,
content rises behind it, the colibrí engraves once at load from a base state
that is already the finished drawing, and a full `prefers-reduced-motion` path
turns all of it off.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Two inks: a dusty periwinkle (`#575892`) for fields, a muted brick (`#A85334`) for marks and rules
- The second ink is screened to a pale tint (`#F7D5C2`) wherever it has to print on the first
- Near-white ivory ground (`#F3F0E7`) with a pure white coated sheet bound inside it
- Montserrat Black all-caps display against Helvetica Neue for everything else
- 3px second-ink register rules, 2px first-ink structural rules, 1px hairlines
- Zero radius, zero shadow, zero gradient; a load-bearing fractal-noise grain (0.85) over all of it
- Mis-registration as the universal state change
- Numbered sections (01–07) earned by a Contents index with dot leaders
- A Video.js player skinned end to end so even third-party chrome prints in-system

## Colors

Two printing inks on near-white ivory stock plus a bound white sheet, with
carbon for text and tinted neutrals pulled from the ground itself — never gray.

### Primary
- **Dusty Periwinkle** (`{colors.ink}`): the field ink, the exact value the user
  specified. It fills whole bands to bleed (the masthead plate, the Practice
  section, the closing contact plate, the player's own ground), draws the 2px
  structural rules, paints the running head's `.past` bottom border, and paints
  the caret, accent and scrollbar. It is also the block a photographic plate is
  mounted on. Measured in the running build: **5.73:1** for periwinkle type on
  the ivory ground, **5.55:1** for cream knocked out of the periwinkle field,
  **6.53:1** for white on it. It is a printed colour, not a near-black, and it
  is the field, not the accent.
- **Second Pass Periwinkle** (`{colors.ink-deep}`): the same plate hit twice.
  It is the document background behind overscroll, the on-ink `::selection`
  foreground, and — since a hover in this world is **more** ink, not less — the
  hover colour for every first-ink link on paper: Contents rows and their dot
  leaders, exhibit titles, colophon links. **7.96:1** on the stock. There is no
  lightened third step: a run-off token existed, only ever held up on ink, and
  was deleted once nothing read it.

### Secondary
- **Soft Brick** (`{colors.orange}`): the second ink, softened at the user's
  request from the minium the two-colour brochures used to a muted, earthier
  brick. It is a **field and mark ink**: the 3px register rules, the
  specification band's bottom rule, section numerals, the 11×2px bullet dashes,
  the 7×7px state squares, the `.u-read` underline, the plate tabs, the
  mis-struck wordmark plate, the player's big play block, progress bar and
  control-bar rule, and both calls to action. Softening it **raised every
  measurement**: white knocked out of it is now **5.31:1**, it reads **4.66:1**
  on the ivory stock and **5.31:1** on the white sheet. On the first ink it
  collapses to **1.23:1**, so it never prints there at full strength.
- **Second Pass Brick** (`{colors.orange-deep}`): the pressed second pass. It is
  the hover and active ground of any second-ink field (`.u-act:hover`,
  `.u-submit:hover`, the player's play block) and the hover text colour of
  `.u-read`. White on it measures **7.18:1**, so a field that deepens on hover
  gains contrast instead of losing it — the system has no lightened hover for
  this ink.
- **Screened Brick** (`{colors.orange-tint}`): **the second ink screened down so
  it reads over the first — not a new accent colour.** This is what a two-colour
  press does when the second ink has to print on the first: it drops the screen
  percentage until the mark lifts. Inside `.u-section--ink > .u-sheet` the remap
  sends **both** `--u-orange` and `--u-orange-deep` to it, so every second-ink
  mark in an ink band — register rule, numeral, bullet dash, state square,
  underline — prints as a pale screen automatically; on an ink ground with no
  remap it is applied by hand, as the form's error message does on `.u-close`.
  Measured on the ink field: **4.74:1**. It is **only** for ink grounds: on the
  ivory stock it measures 1.21:1 and is unusable. Full-strength brick is correct
  on paper and on white.

### Neutral
- **Ivory Litho Stock** (`{colors.paper}`): the page ground — the booklet's
  interior stock, a near-white ivory, not the tan of the scanned cover swatch;
  also the running head's background once it has passed the cover, and the
  colophon band. It only reads as paper because the grain sits over it.
- **Interleaf** (`{colors.paper-lit}`): the half-tint of the stock — the
  player's menu chips.
- **Coated Sheet** (`{colors.white}`): the white pliego bound into the ivory
  stock — the specification band (`.u-spec-band`) and the moving-plate section
  (`.u-section--sheet`). It is also the knockout on the second ink (5.31:1 at
  full strength, 7.18:1 on the second pass), reads 6.53:1 on the first ink, and
  is the white block that handles the player's progress bar.
- **Carbon** (`{colors.carbon}`): body text on stock (12.43:1); the player
  frame and its menu-chip border; the player's time tooltip ground.
- **Carbon Tint** (`{colors.carbon-2}`): labels, captions, table headers, the
  colophon — 6.35:1 on the ivory ground. Tinted toward the stock, not
  desaturated to gray.
- **Cream** (`{colors.cream}`): text, rules and focus rings knocked out of ink,
  **5.55:1** on ink; the cover rule; the player's volume level and times.
- **Cream Tint** (`{colors.cream-2}`): secondary text on ink — labels, captions,
  spec row heads, the cover imprint, form field labels and field rules. It was
  re-derived with the ink: the previous value fell to 4.35:1 on the lighter

### Named Rules
**The Two Ink Rule.** The root prints in exactly two inks — the periwinkle and
the brick — plus the stock and the bound white sheet. Each ink has exactly two
other strengths and no more: `-deep` for a second pass, and (on the second ink)
`-tint` screened down to print on the first. Never add a hue, and never add a
*lightened* step: both `-lit` tokens were tried, only held up on one ground, and
were deleted.

**The Field, Not The Accent Rule.** The first ink is applied in whole bleeding
fields or in structural rules. The second ink is applied in rules, numerals,
marks and solid blocks. Neither is a decorative highlight sprinkled through a
paper section.

**The Screened Second Ink Rule.** The second ink never prints at full strength on
the first — it measures 1.23:1 there. On **any** ink ground it prints as
`{colors.orange-tint}` (4.74:1): inside `.u-section--ink` the remap applies it
automatically, and on an ink ground with no remap (`.u-close`, `.u-plate`) the
component names the tint itself, as the form's error message does. The tint is
the same ink at a lower screen percentage, so it does not count against the Two
Ink Rule — and it runs one way only: on the ivory stock it measures 1.21:1, so
full-strength brick is the only correct choice there.

**The Mark Ink Rule.** The second ink is a field-and-mark ink: rules, numerals,
dashes, squares, underlines, tabs, and solid blocks whose label prints white out
of them (5.31:1). It does not set body, lede, caption, table cell or link text —
the restraint is the world's, not a contrast workaround, and softening the ink
did not loosen it. **Small second-ink text is legal only on the white sheet**,
and the build does it in exactly one place: the Contents numeral inside
`.u-spec-band` (5.31:1 at 13px). That exception is tied to the white ground, not
to the numeral.

**The White Knockout Rule.** Type on a second-ink field is pure white
(`{colors.white}`), never cream and never a dimmed alpha. Hierarchy inside an
second-ink block comes from size and weight — there is deliberately no
`--u-white-dim` token.

**The Tinted Neutral Rule.** Secondary text is tinted from the ground it sits
on (`carbon-2` on stock, `cream-2` on ink). A neutral gray on this page reads as
a different paper and is not permitted.

**The Ink Remap Rule.** A full-bleed ink section does not restyle its children.
`.u-section--ink > .u-sheet` remaps `--u-ink` → cream, `--u-orange` and
`--u-orange-deep` → the screened tint, `--u-carbon` → cream, `--u-carbon-2` →
cream tint, and `--u-hair` to its on-ink alpha, so every component inside flips
automatically. New components must read their colour from these variables so
they inherit the flip for free. The remap lives on the inner `.u-sheet`, never
on the section, so the section keeps its own `--u-ink` as the background. Two
ink grounds deliberately sit outside it — `.u-close` and `.u-plate` set cream
type directly — so a second-ink mark placed on either names the tint itself.

**The Black Halftone Rule.** A photograph prints as a black halftone —
`filter: grayscale(1) contrast(1.12) brightness(1.03)`, dropped to
`filter: none` on hover — and the colour under it comes from the ink block it is
mounted on, never from a tint applied to the pixels. **No `filter: url(#…)` and
no blend-mode duotone on the plates.** Both were built and both failed in the
shipped engines: `mix-blend-mode: screen` and `filter: url(#u-duotone)` each
rendered flat or vanished outright depending on the stacking context and the
viewport size in at least one engine. The `#u-duotone` filter, the `@supports`
guard and the `.u-filters` SVG host are gone from the source; do not
reintroduce them.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Montserrat (self-hosted via `next/font`, exposed as
`--font-montserrat`), weights 800–900, always uppercase
**Body Font:** Helvetica Neue, shipped as a stack (`"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica,
"Helvetica Now Text", Arial, "Liberation Sans", sans-serif`) — it is not
redistributable, so it cannot be self-hosted; it degrades to Helvetica or Arial
off macOS, which is the same grotesque skeleton
**Label/Mono Font:** none. There is no monospace in this world. The only other
loaded face is the Video.js icon font, re-declared from
`/assets/video/font/VideoJS.woff` — a control glyph set, vendored chrome, not a
text face and not a licence for glyph icons on a new surface.

**Character:** Geometric, wide-shouldered 1930s lettering for the headlines
against the neutral grotesque of a spec sheet. Display type is set tight
(negative tracking, line-height below 1) so it reads as a composed block of ink;
everything functional is Helvetica Neue in wide-tracked caps or plain sentence
case, with tabular numerals on every table, the Contents index, the schedule and
the player's time readout.

### Named Rules
**The Two Voices Rule.** Montserrat is for display lines, numerals and the two
button labels only. Every other character on the page — body, lede, caption,
table cell, label, input, nav item — is the Helvetica Neue stack. A third text
family is not available; both faces are pinned in PRODUCT.md.

**The Caps Display Rule.** Display and title type is always uppercase with
negative or near-zero tracking. Sentence-case Montserrat does not exist here.

**The Tracked Caps Rule.** Small functional type earns its hierarchy from
letter-spacing (0.1em–0.2em) and weight, not from size alone. There is no type
below 0.62rem.

**The Covered Ramp Rule.** Every `font-size` in the stylesheet maps to a role in
the hierarchy above. A new literal size is either an existing role or a new
documented role — never an undeclared one-off.

**The Tabular Numerals Rule.** Anything tabular — spec tables, the record table,
the Contents index, the schedule, the player's times — sets
`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, so figures align down a column like a
printed spec.

## Layout

A single centred sheet: max-width 1240px with a fluid gutter
(`{spacing.gutter}`), applied by `.u-sheet` to every band including the ones that
bleed. Sections are vertical bands separated by full-width 3px orange register
rules, each opened by a two-column head (a `4.5rem` numeral column, then the
title and its lede). Seven entries are numbered in the Contents (01 Selected
work, 02 Prototypes, 03 Moving plate, 04 Practice, 05 Record, 06 Schedule,
07 Contact); six of them carry a numeral head and 07 is the closing plate.

The ground alternates on purpose: ivory stock for the catalog bands, the bound
white sheet for the specification band and the moving plate (`.u-section--sheet`),
full-bleed first ink for the masthead, Practice (`.u-section--ink`) and the close.

Rhythm: sections pad `{spacing.section}` vertically (the statement band
`{spacing.statement}`); direct children of a sheet stack at `{spacing.stack}`;
ruled rows (exhibits, departments) pad `{spacing.row}`; the figure and spec
blocks inside the moving plate open at `{spacing.figure}`; table cells pad
9–16px against their hairline. The spec band uses a 3-track asymmetric grid
(`1.25fr / 1fr / minmax(220px, 0.9fr)`), the cover a 2-track
(`1fr / clamp(220px, 30vw, 430px)`), exhibits and departments near-even 2-tracks
with the figure plate leading. The player is fluid, so the moving plate needs no
grid of its own.

Responsive behaviour is collapse, not re-design: 1000px drops the spec grid to
two columns, 880/800/760px collapse the close, exhibit, department and statement
grids to one, 680px finishes the spec band and the schedule rows, 760px turns the
record table into stacked blocks, 520px collapses the paired form fields. The
running head sheds its middle links at 900px and its studio link at 580px; the
cover art reorders above the name at 760px and the imprint is dropped. The
masthead is the only element that owns the first viewport, with
`padding-top: clamp(104px, 13vh, 150px)` clearing the fixed 56px running head.

### Named Rules
**The Band Rule.** New content is a full-width band inside the 1240px sheet,
opened by a `.u-reg` register rule and a numbered `.u-head` — not a floating
module dropped into an existing section.

**The Bound Sheet Rule.** A white band is a sheet bound into the booklet, not a
light theme: it carries the same type, rules and components, and it is reserved
for specification and plate surfaces (`.u-spec-band`, `.u-section--sheet`).

**The Measure Rule.** Prose always carries a measure: 68ch for ledes, 70ch for
body, 58ch for findings, 54–64ch for notes and columns, 34ch for the cover
standfirst, 46ch for the closing paragraph, 640px for the moving plate's spec.
No full-bleed line of text.

## Elevation & Depth

This system has **no shadows** — `box-shadow` appears nowhere in the stylesheet,
and there are no gradients or glows. Depth is printed, not lit. Four devices
carry it: **ink coverage** (a full-bleed periwinkle field reads as nearer than
the stock, the white sheet as a separate leaf), **rules** (3px orange register
rules divide, 2px first-ink rules structure, 1px hairlines group), **mounting**
(a photograph pasted onto a solid block of ink that shows along two of its
edges), and **mis-registration** (a plate struck a few pixels off its mate).

The grain is **load-bearing, not decorative**. `.umbra::before` is a fixed
180px fractal-noise tile at `opacity: 0.85` with the inner rect at alpha 0.16,
multiplied over the whole page. On the near-white ground it is the only thing
that keeps the surface reading as paper instead of as a blank screen: at the old
0.5 / 0.1 strength the lighter stock went flat. Treat its strength as a system
value, not a taste setting — and `@media print` is the one place it is removed,
because paper supplies its own fibre.

### Named Rules
**The No-Shadow Rule.** No element casts a shadow, at any state. If something
must separate from its neighbour, it gets a rule, a hairline, or an ink field.
Hard offset shadows are not an exception; this is not a neobrutalist world.

**The Mis-Registration Rule.** Depth and state changes are expressed as offset,
not elevation: the wordmark's orange ghost plate at `translate(3px, 3px)`, the
masthead's 6px orange run at `translateX(-3px)`, `.u-act:hover` lifting to
`translate(-2px, -2px)`, a halftone plate sliding `translate(5px, 5px)` onto
its mount. Offsets stay in the 2–6px range — press slip, not motion design.

**The Mounted Plate Rule.** A photographic plate is mounted, not lifted. The
ink block behind it (`.u-plate-img::before` at `inset: 14px -14px -14px 14px`,
filled `--u-ink`, with the figure carrying `margin: 0 14px 14px 0`) is the
colour plate the photogravure is pasted onto, revealed 14px along the bottom and
left. It is **not** an offset shadow and must not be refactored into one, given
a blur, a radius, an alpha, or a second offset direction: it is a printed
surface, and it is the only 14px displacement in a system whose state offsets
are 2–6px.

## Shapes

Everything is a rectangle with square corners. `border-radius` is `0` wherever a
browser or a third-party stylesheet would otherwise supply one (focus rings,
inputs, buttons, and every Video.js part: progress holder, play progress, volume
bar and level, menus, tooltips). No token for curvature exists. The form
vocabulary is drawn entirely with strokes: the 3px orange register rule
(`.u-reg`, and the spec band's bottom border) for section breaks and the
player's control-bar top edge; `--u-rule` (2px solid first ink) for table head
underlines, the schedule top edge, the colophon edge and the running head's
bottom once past the cover; `--u-hair` (1px, `rgba(43,43,43,0.26)`, remapped to
`rgba(244,236,217,0.24)` on ink) for table rows and row separators; dotted 1px
leaders joining Contents entries to their labels. Bullet lists replace the disc
with an 11×2px orange dash. A photographic plate has **no frame and no
background of its own**: the 8:5 halftone sits flush on its ink mount, which
reads as a 14px band of solid periwinkle down the left and along the bottom. The
player keeps the 1px carbon frame, and so do its menu chips. The only
non-rectangular shapes on the page are the brand colibrí and the lucide arrow
inside `.u-read`.

Line art is the world's illustration language, and it has a written spec the
user authored for drawings he will draw himself: `DRAWINGS.md` at the repo root,
with `public/assets/drawings/` waiting for them. **No drawing file exists yet and
nothing is wired**, so there is no component for it — but the spec is a form rule
and it binds whatever lands there: SVG with `fill="none"` and
`stroke="currentColor"` so the drawing takes the ink of whatever ground it sits
on, a `viewBox` trimmed to the drawing, no fixed `width`/`height`, at most two
stroke weights (main ≈ viewBox width ÷ 350, detail ≈ ÷ 700), mitre or butt caps
because a technical drawing is square-cornered, and no text inside the file —
callouts stay live HTML so they work in both languages.

### Named Rules
**The Square Corner Rule.** Radius is zero, everywhere, including focus
outlines, form controls and every part of a vendored player. A rounded corner is
off-system.

**The Inherited Ink Rule.** Line art carries no colour of its own. Every drawing
strokes `currentColor` with `fill: none`, so it prints cream on an ink field and
periwinkle on paper from one file — the way the colibrí already does. A drawing
that hardcodes a hue, fills a shape, or sets its own stroke colour is
off-system. Rounded caps are the mark's privilege only: technical drawings take
mitre or butt caps.

**The Stroke Vocabulary Rule.** Use exactly four weights: 3px orange register
rule for section breaks, 2px first-ink rule for structure, 1px hairline for
grouping, 1px dotted for leaders. Don't invent a fifth stroke to signal a fifth
level. Line art is measured separately, relative to its own `viewBox`, not
against this vocabulary.

## Components

The component set is a printed document's furniture: a running head, a spec
table, a Contents index, numbered plates, ruled rows, a moving plate, two
orange blocks and one form. Character: functional, printed, unupholstered.

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square (radius `{rounded.none}`), no border.
- **Primary — the action block** (`.u-act`): a solid second-ink rectangle with
  `{components.button-act.padding}` padding, a Montserrat 900 caps title, a
  white supporting line and a white-underlined mail line (title 18px and
  supporting line 13px both at 5.31:1). It is the only button in the paper half
  of the page.
- **Hover / Focus:** background to `{colors.orange-deep}` and
  `translate(-2px, -2px)` over 0.18s (`:active` returns to rest, and reduced
  motion drops the translate). Focus is a 2px first-ink outline at 3px offset,
  cream when sitting on ink.
- **Submit** (`.u-submit`): full-width second-ink rectangle with white Montserrat
  900 caps tracked 0.08em at 14px (5.31:1), deepening to `{colors.orange-deep}`
  on hover, where the white label reaches 7.18:1; `:disabled` drops to 0.55
  opacity with a `progress` cursor. The hover **deepens** the field — a
  second-ink field never lightens on hover.
- **Text action** (`.u-read`): tracked caps in the first ink (5.73:1) over a 2px
  **second-ink** underline, with a 13px arrow; hovers to
  `{colors.orange-deep}` (6.30:1 on stock). Used for "read the case" / "open the
  document".

### Chips
- **Style** (`.u-state`): a 1px first-ink-bordered rectangle, transparent fill,
  0.62rem caps tracked 0.16em, preceded by a 7×7px solid **second-ink** square.
  On an ink field the remap prints the border and label in cream (5.55:1) and the
  square in the screened tint.
- **State:** `.u-state--live` pulses that square's opacity (1 → 0.28, 2.6s,
  infinite) and holds still under reduced motion. Chips report status; they are
  never filters or actions.

### Cards / Containers
There are **no cards**. Grouping is done by ruled rows: exhibits and departments
are grid rows separated by hairlines inside a hairline-topped container, tables
by hairline rows under a 2px first-ink head. The closest thing to a container is
the player frame (`.u-player`): a 1px carbon border, periwinkle background,
square corners, no padding, content flush to the frame. The plate
(`.u-plate-img`) is not a container either — it is a photograph on a mount,
borderless and without a background of its own.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** ruled ledger fields on the closing ink plate — transparent
  background, no box, a single 1px cream-tint bottom rule, cream text at
  0.98rem, `{components.field-input.padding}` padding, radius 0. The label above
  is 0.62rem caps tracked 0.18em in cream-tint (4.73:1). The select drops its
  native appearance; its options revert to carbon-on-paper because the OS paints
  them.
- **Focus:** the bottom rule goes cream and thickens to 2px; keyboard focus adds
  a 2px cream outline at 4px offset.
- **Error / Status:** the form message sets `{colors.orange-tint}` on error
  (4.74:1) and cream on success (5.55:1). The closing plate is an ink ground
  with no remap, so the error line names the screened tint directly — this is
  The Screened Second Ink Rule applied by hand, and it is the pattern for any
  second-ink mark on `.u-close` or `.u-plate`. A honeypot field is positioned
  off-canvas.

### Navigation
- **Style** (`.u-run`): fixed 56px running head, transparent over the masthead
  with cream type; once scrolled past the cover (`.past`) it takes the paper
  ground, carbon type and a 2px first-ink bottom rule, transitioning colour over
  0.3s.
- **Typography:** the mark is Montserrat 800 caps 0.82rem tracked 0.14em beside a
  30×20px colibrí; links are Helvetica Neue 600 caps 0.68rem tracked 0.16em.
- **States:** hover draws a 2px `currentColor` underline (a transparent border is
  reserved at rest so nothing shifts). Focus rings follow the bar's own colour —
  cream over the cover, the first ink once past it.
- **Mobile:** middle links disappear at 900px, the studio link at 580px, where
  the mark also shrinks. The language toggle (`.u-lang`) is a 1px
  `currentColor` box holding two 0.64rem caps buttons, the active one at full
  opacity and underlined.

### The moving plate (signature)
Section 03 is a Video.js 8 player (`app/PlatePlayer.tsx`, a dynamic import)
wrapped in `.u-player` and skinned part by part so no vendored chrome survives.
The default skin is round, translucent and from another world; every piece is
replaced.

- **Frame:** 1px carbon border, periwinkle ground, fluid width, poster filling
  the frame at `object-fit: cover`.
- **Play block:** a square `clamp(64px, 7vw, 88px)` orange block with a white
  glyph, centred (`vjs-big-play-centered`), going `{colors.orange-deep}` on

- **Control bar as the plate's footer:** 50px tall, periwinkle, **forced visible
  and opaque at rest** because it is part of the composition, with a 3px orange

- **Progress:** an 8px holder on veiled cream, played time in orange, buffer at
  `rgba(244,236,217,0.4)`, and a 10×14px pure-white block as the handle. The

- **Times:** Montserrat 700 caps 0.72rem tabular in cream; the remaining-time

- **Volume:** square bar and level, cream on veiled cream.
- **Menus:** paper chips — `paper-lit` ground, 1px carbon border, square,

- **Focus:** 2px cream outline inset 2px, so it reads against the periwinkle

- **Media:** `/public/assets/video/reel.mp4` + `reel-poster.jpg`, transcoded with

#### Named Rules
**The Beat-The-Package Rule.** Every player selector is prefixed
`.u-player .video-js` on purpose. `video-js.css` loads *after* this stylesheet
because the player is a dynamic import, so the skin wins on specificity, not on
order. Do not "simplify" these selectors and do not rely on import order.

**The Served Icon Font Rule.** The Video.js icon font is re-declared with
`@font-face` from `/assets/video/font/VideoJS.woff` with `font-display: block`,
because the package stylesheet's relative font path does not resolve through the
bundler and the controls render as tofu. Keep the file in `/public` and keep the
absolute path. This is a repair to vendored chrome, not permission to draw this
system's own icons from a glyph font.

### Other signature components
- **The register rule** (`.u-reg`): a 3px full-width second-ink bar opening every

  at `scaleX(0)` and sweeps from the left over 0.62s; the content behind it rises
  16px into place with staggered `--u-delay`. Without JS, or under reduced
  motion, everything renders at rest.
- **The specification band** (`.u-spec-band`): the white sheet directly under
  the cover, closed by a 3px second-ink rule; it carries the spec table, the
  Contents index and the action block.
- **The spec table** (`.u-spec`): a 9.5rem caps header column against

- **The Contents index** (`.u-contents`): hairline rows, a Montserrat 900

  leader both deepen to `{colors.ink-deep}` on hover (7.96:1). It is what earns

- **The mounted plate** (`.u-plate-img` + `.u-plate-no`): the booklet's own way

  running head, 34×22px in the colophon, 92px wide as a department mark. At load
  it engraves once: each path's measured length is passed in as `--len` and the
  base state is already the finished drawing, so if the animation never runs the
  bird still reads.

### Named Rules
**The Print State Rule.** `@media print` hides the running head, the form and the
grain, and returns the page to black on white — the artifact's own admission that
it is a document.

**The Browser Chrome Rule.** Selection, caret, accent and scrollbar colours are
part of the system (first ink on paper, cream selection on ink). A new ink
surface restates its `::selection` pair, and it reads the pair from `--u-ink`,
never from a literal.

**The Dressed Vendor Rule.** A third-party widget is admitted only fully
dressed: square corners, this palette, this type, this focus ring, and its own
spec table beside it. A default skin is never shipped, not even partially.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** scope every new rule under `.umbra`. Nothing in this system may leak to
  `/lumen`, `/admin` or any legacy route.
- **Do** read colour from `--u-ink`, `--u-orange`, `--u-carbon`, `--u-carbon-2`
  and `--u-hair` so a component flips automatically inside `.u-section--ink`.
- **Do** print the second ink as `--u-orange-tint` wherever it has to sit on the
  first ink, and at full strength on paper and on the white sheet.
- **Do** keep the second ink to fields, rules, numerals, marks and solid blocks,
  and print white out of it when it carries a label.
- **Do** open a new section with a `.u-reg` register rule and a numbered
  `.u-head`, and add its row to the Contents index — the numbering has to stay
  earned.
- **Do** put specification and plate surfaces on the white sheet
  (`.u-spec-band`, `.u-section--sheet`) and keep the rest on the ivory stock.
- **Do** print a specification. New proof arrives as a hairline spec table, a
  caption under its title, or a dashed findings list, with its real numbers.
- **Do** keep type to Montserrat caps for display and the Helvetica Neue stack
  for everything else, with tabular numerals on anything columnar, and map every
  new size to a documented role.
- **Do** express state as mis-registration in the 2–6px range, plus a colour
  change to `ink-deep` or `orange-deep`. A hover in this world is **more** ink,
  never a lighter tint.
- **Do** mount a photograph: a borderless 8:5 black halftone over a solid ink
  block showing 14px on two edges, with a numbered orange tab.
- **Do** keep the grain at full strength (0.85 over a 0.16 rect). It is what
  makes the near-white ground read as stock.
- **Do** draw this system's own icons and line art as inline or referenced SVG
  with `fill="none"` and `stroke="currentColor"`, the way the colibrí and the
  `.u-read` arrow are drawn, and follow `DRAWINGS.md` for anything that lands in
  `public/assets/drawings/`.
- **Do** ship every motion with a `prefers-reduced-motion` off-switch and a
  base state that already looks finished.
- **Do** dress a vendored widget end to end, and beat its stylesheet on
  specificity rather than on load order.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a third ink, and don't add a lightened strength. Two inks plus
  the stock and the white sheet; `-deep` and `-tint` are strengths of those two,
  not new hues. Both `-lit` tokens were shipped, each held up on only one
  ground, and both are deleted — reintroducing one is a regression.
- **Don't** print the second ink at full strength on the first — it measures
  1.23:1. The screened tint (4.74:1) is the answer: the remap applies it inside
  `.u-section--ink`, and on `.u-close` or `.u-plate` the component names it.
  Equally, don't put the tint on paper — 1.21:1. Small second-ink text is legal
  only on the white sheet (5.31:1), where the Contents numeral is the single
  shipped case; keep body, lede, caption, label, cell and link text out of the
  second ink regardless of the measured ratio.
- **Don't** treat `--u-orange-tint` as a new accent. It is the second ink
  screened down for one job: reading over the first ink.
- **Don't** take the first ink back toward a near-black. Its lightness is the
  decision — it reads as a printed colour and sits near the 1933 cover's own ink.
- **Don't** dim white with opacity on a second-ink field. Size and weight carry the
  hierarchy; there is no `--u-white-dim`.
- **Don't** add `border-radius`, `box-shadow`, a gradient or a glow anywhere,
  including inside a vendored player's parts — and not a hard offset shadow
  either; the plate's ink mount is a printed surface, not a shadow.
- **Don't** introduce a monospace, a serif, or a system display face. There are
  two text families, both pinned in PRODUCT.md; the only extra face is the
  player's vendored icon font, which is not a precedent for glyph icons.
- **Don't** build a card, a tile grid of equal previews, or a rounded panel.
  Grouping is hairline-ruled rows.
- **Don't** add a kicker or eyebrow above a heading. `.u-head` is a numeral
  beside its title; the label primitive is for fields and captions, not for
  announcing a heading.
- **Don't** use gray for secondary text. Tint it from the ground.
- **Don't** publish a photograph in full colour at rest. It prints as a black
  halftone on its ink mount, behind a numbered plate tab; full colour is the
  hover state only.
- **Don't** reintroduce `filter: url(#…)` or a blend-mode duotone on the plates.
  `mix-blend-mode: screen` and `#u-duotone` were both shipped and both broke —
  flat or invisible depending on stacking context and viewport in at least one
  engine. Flat CSS filter functions only.
- **Don't** read the plate's ink block as a hard offset shadow, and don't
  refactor it into one. No blur, no alpha, no radius, no second direction: it is
  the colour plate the photograph is pasted onto.
- **Don't** take the ground back toward tan, and don't weaken the grain to
  compensate for a lighter ground. The stock is `{colors.paper}` and the grain
  is what gives it body.
- **Don't** hardcode a colour literal. The stylesheet now has none outside the
  token declarations, and the on-ink `::selection` pair — the last holdout —
  reads `--u-ink-deep`. Keep it that way.
- **Don't** let a vendored control bar auto-hide, go translucent, or keep its
  own icon-font path; the bar is the plate's printed footer.
- **Don't** ship sample media as work. Footage that is not client work carries
  "sample footage" in its caption, as `/assets/video/reel.mp4` does.
- **Don't** raise the specificity of `.umbra :where(a)` or delete
  `.umbra #contact.u-close` or `html:has(.umbra)`; all three exist to keep the
  legacy `globals.css` from repainting this page.
- **Don't** port these tokens into `globals.css` or "unify" the other routes.
  Their separate system is the user's decision, not drift.
